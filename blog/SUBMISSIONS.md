## 1. JobSearchDB (bez maksas)
Forma: https://www.jobsearchdb.com/submissions

- **Name of job board:** QA Job Board
- **Type of job search resource:** Job Board
- **URL:** https://www.qajobboard.com
- **What type of job seekers would this help the most?**
  > Software testers and quality assurance professionals: QA engineers, QA automation engineers, SDETs (software development engineers in test), manual testers, QA analysts, performance testers, test leads and QA managers. Most listings are remote (around 90%), with the largest number of roles in the United States, India, Canada, Poland, the UK and Latin America. It covers junior to lead level and is useful for people who want only software testing roles, without the factory, food safety or call-centre "quality" jobs that general job sites mix in.
- **Optional description and notes:**
  > Launched in 2024. A niche job board for software testing and QA roles only, with around 1,000 open jobs at any time from about 700 companies. Most jobs come directly from company career pages and applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters) and from public remote job sources. Employers can also post jobs directly. Each job shows the advertised salary when the employer publishes one, and for remote jobs it shows which countries or regions the company can hire in. Jobs are refreshed daily and removed when they close. Free for job seekers, including email job alerts. The blog publishes QA salary and skills data based on the live listings.
- **AI disclosure:**
  > No generative AI is used to create or rewrite job listings. Jobs are collected from employers' own career pages with scripts and shown as published. AI is used to help write the blog and to sort jobs into role categories by title.
- **I am:** a company admin or representative
- **Is it free?:** Some features are free (job seekers use it for free; employers pay to post)
- **Your name / email:** tavi dati

> Pārbaudi gadu „Launched in 2024”. Search Console rāda, ka vietne ir redzama vismaz kopš 2024. gada oktobra. Ja zini precīzāku datumu, nomaini.

## 2. FindaJobBoard
Vispirms izveido kontu https://findajobboard.com/submit, tad aizpildi kartiņu.

- **Board name:** QA Job Board
- **Website:** https://www.qajobboard.com
- **Category / industry:** Tech Job Boards (papildus: Remote Job Boards)
- **Tags:** QA, software testing, SDET, test automation, remote jobs, quality assurance
- **RSS feed (darbu rādīšanai):** https://www.qajobboard.com/jobs.rss
- **Short description (1 teikums):**
  > The job board for software testers: QA, SDET and test automation jobs from company career pages, updated daily.
- **Long description:**
  > QA Job Board lists only software testing and quality assurance roles, so testers don't have to filter out unrelated "quality" jobs. There are around 1,000 open jobs from about 700 companies, including QA engineers, QA automation engineers, SDETs, manual testers, QA analysts, performance testers, QA leads and test managers. Around 90% of the jobs are remote, and each remote job shows where the company can hire, such as a single country, Europe or LATAM. Salaries are shown whenever the employer publishes them. The median advertised QA salary on the board is $110,000.
  >
  > Job seekers browse and apply for free and can get new QA jobs by email. Employers can post a QA job from $99 for 30 days, or $199 for a featured post that is pinned to the top, included in the job email and shared on LinkedIn.
- **For employers / pricing:** Standard $99 (30 days), Featured $199
- **Logo:** to pašu, ko izmanto Jobboardly/LinkedIn lapā
