> Hi Product Hunt! 👋
>
> I'm Mārcis, and I built QA Job Board because finding a software testing job on general job sites is painful. Search "QA" and you get factory inspectors, food safety auditors and call-centre quality roles mixed in with the jobs testers actually want.
>
> **What it does**
> • Lists only software testing roles: QA engineers, SDETs, test automation, manual testers, QA leads and test managers
> • 2,000+ open jobs from 1,200+ companies, collected from company career pages every day
> • Shows the salary whenever the employer publishes it
> • Shows where a remote job is really open: "remote" usually means one country, and we show which one (or Europe, LATAM, worldwide)
> • Free for job seekers, with email alerts
>
> **Some things we learned from the data**
> • The median advertised QA salary is $110k, and SDETs earn the most among hands-on roles
> • Selenium is still the most requested tool, but Playwright is close behind (31% vs 27% of job posts)
> • Only ~4% of QA jobs are junior, so we wrote a roadmap for people trying to break in
>
> **For hiring teams:** you can post a QA job from $99 and reach testers who are actively looking.
>
> I'd love your feedback: what would make this more useful for your job search or hiring? 🙏

## Komentāru atbildes (sagataves)
- **„How is this different from LinkedIn/Indeed?”**
  > LinkedIn has more jobs overall, but you have to filter out a lot of non-software "QA" roles and reposts. We only list software testing jobs, link straight to the company's own application page and show salary and remote location rules on every job.
- **„Where do the jobs come from?”**
  > Mostly from companies' own career pages and applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters), plus a few remote job sources. Jobs are refreshed daily and removed when they close. Employers can also post directly.
- **„Is it free?”**
  > Yes, for job seekers, including job alerts. Employers pay from $99 to post a job.
- **„Do you have remote jobs in Europe / my country?”**
  > Yes. On the remote page each job shows where the company can hire. We also have country pages, for example /s/poland-jobs and /s/germany-jobs.
