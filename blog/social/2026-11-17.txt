Selenium is in 30.4% of QA job posts. Playwright is in 27.0%. In automation and SDET roles, Playwright is already ahead.

We compared the three big frameworks across 1,075 open QA jobs on QA Job Board:

- Automation and SDET posts: Playwright 51.8%, Selenium 45.3%, Cypress 31.2%.
- Selenium still goes with Java (41.6% of its posts). TypeScript is twice as common in Playwright posts as in Selenium posts.
- Cypress rarely stands alone: only 7.9% of Cypress posts mention neither Playwright nor Selenium.
- Pay barely changes by framework: medians of $108k (Selenium), $105k (Playwright) and $102k (Cypress). Role and seniority matter far more.
