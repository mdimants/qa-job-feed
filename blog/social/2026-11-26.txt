We counted 175 open QA jobs limited to candidates in Latin America. Every single one is remote.

Brazil leads with 53 roles from 35 companies, then Mexico (36) and Colombia (19). Another 29 accept anyone in the region.
Most of the hiring comes from nearshore firms placing testers on US client teams, plus product companies such as Airbnb, Upgrade and Onfleet.
English matters: 97 of the 175 ads mention it, and 49 ask for an advanced or fluent level.
Playwright (74 ads) edges out Selenium (71) in the region, and 37 of the jobs are contracts.

If you test software from Latin America, do you work for a local company or a US client?
