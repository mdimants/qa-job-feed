Only 17% of QA jobs in Germany are fully remote. We looked at all 141 open roles from 96 companies.

What stood out:
- 38% of descriptions ask for German language skills.
- ISTQB is mentioned in 36% of listings, far more than in other markets.
- Software Tester (32%) and Test Manager (25%) are the biggest role groups.
- Only 5% of German listings publish a salary.

If you are looking for a QA job in Germany: get ISTQB Foundation, state your German level clearly, and show one small automation project.
