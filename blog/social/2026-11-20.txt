Only 47 of 1,130 open QA jobs on our board are tagged junior. Here is where the entry-level roles actually are.

We read every live listing for junior, intern, graduate and fresher titles and found 56 real entry-level software testing jobs from 48 companies.

40 of the 56 are remote. 20 are limited to US candidates.
The median advertised junior QA salary is $85,000, from a small sample of 11 listings.
SQL shows up in 38% of entry-level descriptions and API testing in 32%, more than Selenium (23%) or Playwright (16%).
ISTQB is mentioned in only 9%.
