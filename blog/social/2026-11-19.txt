Only 49 of 1,119 open QA jobs today are junior roles. That is 4%.

We looked at every fresh quality assurance job on QA Job Board this week. A few things stood out:

- 755 companies are hiring, and 91.8% of the jobs can be done remotely
- The median advertised salary is $110,000; QA analysts sit lower at $91,000, test managers at $163,000
- QA Engineer, QA Automation Engineer and Senior QA Engineer make up more than half of all openings
- Selenium is still in 29.5% of job descriptions, but Playwright is close behind at 25.5%

One more thing: about half of the job titles with "Quality Assurance" in them do not mention testing, software or engineering. Many are pharma or operations QA. Search by role name instead.
