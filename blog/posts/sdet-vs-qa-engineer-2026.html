An SDET (Software Development Engineer in Test) is a QA engineer who is hired mainly to write code: test frameworks, CI pipelines and test tooling. A QA engineer is hired to own product quality, and coding is one of several tools for that. In our November 2026 data, SDET posts pay more (a median of $121,000 a year against $98,000 for QA engineer posts) and ask for programming and DevOps skills two to four times as often. QA engineer roles are far more common: 336 open posts against 69 for SDETs.

If you already know which side you are on, browse SDET jobs or QA engineer jobs on QA Job Board. The rest of this article shows how the two roles differ in real job posts.

How many SDET and QA engineer jobs are there?

QA Job Board lists 1,204 open QA jobs from 811 companies today. QA Engineer is the largest job category with 336 posts. Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) is the sixth largest with 69 posts, behind QA Automation Engineer (212), Senior QA Engineer (142), Software Tester (135) and QA Analyst (91).

So for every SDET post there are about five QA engineer posts. That gap matters if you are choosing what to call yourself on a CV. Many companies hire people with SDET skills under the title "QA Automation Engineer" or "Senior QA Engineer", so the SDET title alone undercounts the demand for coding testers. If you have SDET skills, it is worth searching QA automation engineer jobs as well.

The SDET title is also more concentrated at large software companies. In the 64 SDET posts in our feed, the companies with the most openings were Okta (4) and Keeper Security (3). The QA engineer list is spread across far more employers: 322 posts from 263 companies, including outsourcing firms, healthcare companies and retailers.

1. Programming is the job, not a bonus

Half of SDET posts name Python, half name JavaScript and 56% name TypeScript. In QA engineer posts, no language appears in more than 19% of descriptions. Many QA engineer posts list coding as "nice to have"; SDET posts expect you to build and maintain the framework itself. The word "framework" appears in 92% of SDET descriptions.

2. CI/CD and infrastructure

84% of SDET posts mention CI/CD, and 47% name GitHub Actions specifically, against 8% of QA engineer posts. Docker (41%), AWS (39%) and Kubernetes (22%) also appear far more often. An SDET is usually expected to make tests run on every pull request, in containers, and to keep the pipeline fast. That is closer to platform engineering than to classic testing.

3. Modern automation tools

Playwright leads in both groups, but at very different levels: 59% of SDET posts against 25% of QA engineer posts. Across all QA jobs, Selenium (29.3%) still appears slightly more often than Playwright (26.3%), so SDET roles are where newer tools are adopted first. Our comparison of Playwright, Selenium and Cypress goes deeper into tool demand. You can also search Playwright jobs directly.

What they have in common

The two roles overlap more than the titles suggest. Half of SDET posts still mention test plans or test cases, and one in five mentions manual testing, almost the same as QA engineer posts (18%). Employers want SDETs who can think like testers, not only developers who write tests. QA engineer posts, meanwhile, ask for Jira (21%) and SQL (19%) more often than for any programming language. These posts weight test design, bug reporting and working with product teams more heavily.

Which role is more senior?

We classified seniority from words in the job title. Of the 64 SDET posts, 26 were senior and 8 were lead or manager roles, so more than half sit above mid-level. Only 2 were junior. Of the 322 QA engineer posts, 260 had no seniority marker (mid-level), 52 were lead or manager roles and 10 were junior.

The practical lesson: SDET is rarely an entry-level title. Most people get there after a few years as a QA engineer or QA automation engineer, or move across from software development. If you are starting out, our guide to getting your first QA job is a better first step.

Are SDET jobs remote?

Both roles are mostly remote. 91.7% of all QA jobs on the board are remote. In our feed, 89.1% of SDET posts and 93.5% of QA engineer posts were remote. The United States had the most posts in both groups (22 SDET and 106 QA engineer posts), followed for SDETs by Canada (11) and India (9). Many remote roles are limited to one country, so check the location line before you apply. See all remote QA jobs or our list of companies hiring remote SDETs.

SDET or QA engineer: which should you choose?

Choose the SDET path if you enjoy writing code more than designing test cases, and if you are comfortable owning a framework, a CI pipeline and code reviews. You compete for fewer openings, but the pay is higher and the skills transfer to software engineering.

Choose the QA engineer path if you like working across the product, finding the bugs other people miss and shaping how a team tests. There are about five times as many openings, the role is open to people without a computer science degree, and you can add automation over time. Many QA engineers move into SDET or QA automation roles after two or three years.

If you want to move from QA engineer to SDET, the job posts point to a clear order: learn one language well (TypeScript or Python), build a small Playwright framework with API tests, run it in GitHub Actions with Docker, and put it on GitHub. That covers most of the gap in the table above. Companies that hire for these roles often appear on our list of the best software companies to work for.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an SDET and a QA engineer?

An SDET is a developer who specialises in testing and spends most of the time writing test code, frameworks and CI pipelines. A QA engineer owns product quality through test design, manual and automated testing and bug reporting. In our data, 84% of SDET posts mention CI/CD against 30% of QA engineer posts.

Does an SDET earn more than a QA engineer?

Yes, usually. The median advertised SDET salary is $121,000 a year against $98,000 for QA engineers, based on 14 and 64 posts with a published salary. Senior QA engineers earn a similar amount to SDETs, with a median of $128,000.

Is SDET harder than QA?

It requires more programming. Half or more of SDET posts name Python, JavaScript or TypeScript, while no language appears in more than 19% of QA engineer posts. Most SDET posts are mid-level or senior, and only 2 of 64 were junior roles.

Can a QA engineer become an SDET?

Yes, and it is the most common path. Learn one language, build a test framework with Playwright and API tests, and run it in CI. Playwright appears in 59% of SDET posts, so it is a good tool to start with.

Where can I find SDET jobs?

Browse SDET jobs on QA Job Board, which has 69 open SDET posts today. Also search QA automation engineer jobs, because many companies hire SDET profiles under that title.

Methodology

Board-wide figures (1,204 open QA jobs, 811 companies, job counts by category, salary percentiles, tool shares and the 91.7% remote share) come from QA Job Board data collected on 28 November 2026. The skill comparison uses the 64 SDET and 322 QA engineer posts with full descriptions in our job feed on the same day, matched by keyword on the description text; seniority is based on words in the job title. Salaries use the midpoint of each published range, converted to US dollars a year.
