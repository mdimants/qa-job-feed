Software testing jobs split into three tracks that hire very differently: manual and general software tester roles, QA automation engineer roles, and SDET roles. Right now QA Job Board lists 157 manual and general tester jobs, 196 QA automation engineer jobs and 66 SDET jobs. Automation is the biggest single track, manual testing is far more often a contract, and SDET roles ask for the most programming. Pay rises as you move from manual testing to automation, but the gap is smaller than many people expect.

Manual and general software tester jobs

The "Software Tester" category is the broadest one. It covers titles like QA Tester, Software Tester, Mobile QA Tester and Systems Integration Tester, and it also catches roles that are not classic web or app testing. When we looked at the 105 Software Tester listings that were still live when we wrote this, 19 were game or localization testing jobs (14 of them from Pole To Win International), and 11 had "mobile" or "app" in the title. A few were hardware or inspection roles that use the word "testing". If you want software QA specifically, read the title and the first paragraph before you apply.

Two things stand out about this track:

  • Contracts are common. 34 of those 105 Software Tester listings were contracts, about a third. Across the whole board, 171 of 1,119 jobs are contracts, roughly 15%. For the 35 live QA Manual Tester listings, 15 were contracts.
  • The tool list is short. Among Software Tester job descriptions, Jira appeared in about 20% and SQL in about 17%. Selenium appeared in only about 14%, compared with 29.5% across all QA jobs.

Dedicated QA Manual Tester roles look different. They are fewer, but their descriptions are more demanding than the title suggests. Postman appeared in about 69% of them, Jira in 66% and SQL in 51%. Playwright, Cypress and Selenium each appeared in 40%. So a "manual" tester job today often means manual exploratory testing plus API checks, some SQL and the ability to read or extend an existing automated suite. Flosum alone had 10 of these listings, so the category is small and one employer can move the numbers. Browse current manual QA tester jobs.

QA automation engineer jobs

QA Automation Engineer is the second largest role category on the board after QA Engineer, with 196 open jobs. Almost all of these are full-time: 157 of the 177 listings live when we wrote this, with only 18 contracts. The track is also more senior. 61 of those 177 had "Senior" in the title, while only 2 were junior.

The tools are what you would expect, but the split is close:

  • Playwright appeared in about 53% of automation engineer descriptions and Selenium in about 49%.
  • Cypress appeared in about 31%, Postman in 30% and Jenkins in 24%.
  • Python, JavaScript and Java each appeared in roughly a third of descriptions, with TypeScript close behind at 29%.

In other words, there is no single winning stack. If you know one browser framework well and one language that matches it (Playwright with TypeScript, or Selenium with Java), you meet the core requirement for most of these jobs. Our comparison of Playwright vs Selenium vs Cypress breaks the demand down further. Browse QA automation engineer jobs or search for Selenium jobs directly.

SDET jobs

SDET (Software Development Engineer in Test) roles are the smallest of the three tracks, with 66 open jobs, but they ask for the most engineering work. In the 50 SDET descriptions live when we wrote this:

  • Python appeared in 64%, JavaScript in 62%, TypeScript in 56% and Java in 48%. Many SDET posts ask for more than one language.
  • GitHub Actions appeared in 46%, and AWS and Docker in 42% each. Automation engineer posts mention these much less often (20% for GitHub Actions, 18% for Docker).
  • k6 appeared in 28%, so performance testing is a regular part of the job.

SDET roles are also the most senior: 22 of the 50 had "Senior" in the title and 7 were lead roles. Only 2 were junior. If you are moving from automation into SDET, the gap is usually CI pipelines, cloud basics and writing test tooling, not a new test framework. See current SDET jobs or our guide to remote SDET jobs.

Are software testing jobs remote?

Mostly, yes. 91.8% of all jobs on QA Job Board are remote, and the testing tracks follow the same pattern. About 91% of live Software Tester listings and about 93% of QA Automation Engineer listings were remote. Every QA Manual Tester listing we checked was remote. Many remote jobs are limited to one country or region, though. For Software Tester roles, the United States was named most often (32 listings), followed by India (7) and Canada (5). Manual tester roles were more European, with Poland named 6 times. Filter by location on the remote QA jobs page.

Which track should you aim for?

  • New to testing: general software tester and manual roles are the easiest way in, but junior titles are rare across the board (49 of 1,119 jobs). Learn Jira, SQL and Postman first, then one automation framework. Our junior QA roadmap covers the steps.
  • Manual tester with a year or two of experience: automation is the largest pool of full-time jobs. Pick Playwright or Selenium and build one public project with it.
  • Automation engineer: SDET roles pay at the top end. Add CI (GitHub Actions or Jenkins), Docker and a second language.

What is the difference between a QA automation engineer and an SDET?

An SDET usually writes more code and owns more of the test infrastructure. In our data, 46% of SDET descriptions mention GitHub Actions and 42% mention Docker, compared with 20% and 18% for QA automation engineer roles. The median salaries are similar, at $112,000 for SDET and $114,000 for automation.

Are software testing jobs remote?

Methodology

Board-wide counts, role counts, tool shares and salaries come from QA Job Board's daily insights file for 19 November 2026 (1,119 jobs). Per-role breakdowns of contract share, seniority, tools and locations come from a separate pass over the same day's feed, covering the listings still live at the time of writing (105 Software Tester, 35 QA Manual Tester, 177 QA Automation Engineer and 50 SDET jobs). Salaries are the midpoint of the published range, converted to annual US dollars, and groups with fewer than 5 salaries are not shown.
