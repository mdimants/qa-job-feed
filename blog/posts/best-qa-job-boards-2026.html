Why a niche QA job board helps

When you search "QA" on a general job site, you also get quality inspectors, food safety auditors and call-centre quality analysts. A niche board only lists software testing roles. On QA Job Board, every job falls into one of these categories:

Two more things matter for testers. First, location rules: most remote QA jobs are limited to one country, and QA Job Board shows where each employer can hire. Second, salary: 17.4% of our QA jobs publish pay, and the median is $110,000 per year. See the QA salary report.

How to use several job boards without wasting time

  1. Pick one niche board as your main source and check it every day. New QA jobs get most applications in the first week. We add about 200 new QA jobs a week.
  2. Set alerts on LinkedIn for your exact title and country, and switch off "similar jobs".
  3. Use the company's own careers page to apply whenever you can. Most jobs on QA Job Board link straight to the employer's applicant tracking system.
  4. Join one community such as Ministry of Testing or a local testing meetup. Referrals still beat cold applications.
  5. Avoid pay-per-bug platforms if you want a full-time role. They rarely lead to a permanent job.
