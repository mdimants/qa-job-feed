How many QA jobs are open in India?

In today's board-wide snapshot of 1,219 open QA jobs, 109 are tagged with India as a location. Only the United States has more (404). Canada (68) and Poland (48) follow.

For this article we went one step further and pulled every listing posted since 1 October 2026 whose location limit is India. After removing non-software roles such as bank audit and proofreading positions, 137 jobs were left. The difference from 109 comes from the wider date window and the stricter location filter.

Three things stand out in those 137 jobs:

  • All of them are remote. Board-wide, 91.8% of QA jobs are remote, so this is not unusual for our listings, but it means you do not need to live in Bengaluru or Pune to apply. Bengaluru is still the city mentioned most often in the descriptions, followed by Chennai and Pune, usually as the base for an occasional office visit.
  • Most are full-time. 124 are full-time and 13 (about 9%) are contracts.
  • Employers are spread out. 91 companies posted the 137 jobs, so no single firm dominates.

Who hires remote QA engineers in India?

The employers fall into three groups.

IT services and consulting firms post the most roles. Photon has 13 open "QA Engineer | Offshore" and QA Lead roles, many of them for US healthcare and retail clients. PradeepIT Consulting has 8 (automation testers, API testers, a C# automation tester and a QA team lead), and Valtech, Vrinda International, Ciklum and ITHR Technologies have 3 or 4 each. These roles often name the client's stack in the title, such as Duck Creek, Guidewire, Salesforce or SAP.

Foreign product companies hiring in India are the second group. HighLevel has 4 SDET roles, from Senior SDET for performance testing up to Director of SDET. SupplyHouse.com, Labcorp, YipitData, Sage, JumpCloud, AlphaSense, Dayforce and Deque Systems also have open India-remote QA roles. These are the jobs where pay is most often benchmarked against international rates.

Indian startups make up the rest: Sprinto (Lead SDET), Drivetrain (QA intern), AccuKnox (QA trainee), Girman Technologies and Shuru Technologies, among others.

How much experience do you need?

More than many candidates expect. 98 of the 137 ads state a minimum number of years. Five years is the most common requirement (26 ads), and 72 of those 98 ads ask for five years or more. Only 26 accept under five years.

Seniority in the titles tells the same story. About 61 titles read as mid-level, 42 as senior and 28 as lead, manager or director. Only about 6 are clearly junior, intern or trainee roles, including a QA intern at Drivetrain and a QA trainee at AccuKnox. Across the whole board, 54 of 1,219 jobs are junior. If you are just starting, read our guide to entry-level QA jobs and check the junior QA engineer jobs page often, because these roles fill quickly.

How much do remote QA jobs in India pay?

There is not enough public data for a reliable median. Only 9 of the 137 India ads publish pay, and they mix rupees, US dollars and one hourly contract. Here are the published figures, quoted as posted:

  • QA Engineer at Girman Technologies (6 months to 2 years of experience): 3 to 6 LPA.
  • Quality Manager & Automation Tester at Pontoonglobal: INR 7 to 15 lakh a year.
  • Duck Creek QA Analyst at Vrinda International: INR 16 lakh a year.
  • QA Engineer at SupplyHouse.com: USD 18,482 to 27,723 a year.
  • QA Engineer Team Lead at SupplyHouse.com: USD 29,000 to 36,000 a year.
  • Software Tester at Mercor: up to USD 60 an hour, as a contract.

For comparison, the board-wide median advertised QA salary is $111,000, but that figure is driven by US roles. Indian salaries for the same title are much lower in absolute terms, so compare offers against local rates and the full package (health insurance, paid leave, equipment) rather than against US numbers. How to stand out for remote QA jobs from India

  1. Lead with API and automation work. API testing appears in 58% of these ads. A small public project with Postman collections or REST Assured tests, plus a UI suite in Selenium or Playwright, covers the most common requests.
  2. Show CI/CD. 41% of the jobs mention a pipeline. Run your test suite in GitHub Actions or Jenkins and link the green build in your CV.
  3. Name the domain. Consulting roles often require a specific platform (Salesforce, Guidewire, Duck Creek, SAP). If you have tested one, put it in your headline.
  4. Be clear about working hours. Remote roles for US and UK clients sometimes expect a few hours of overlap. Say in your application which hours you can cover.
  5. Prepare for a practical round. Product companies usually include a coding or test-design exercise. Our QA automation interview questions cover the common ones.

It is possible but hard. Only about 6 of the 137 India roles are junior, intern or trainee positions, and 72 of the 98 ads that state experience ask for five years or more. Internships and trainee roles at startups are the most realistic entry point.
