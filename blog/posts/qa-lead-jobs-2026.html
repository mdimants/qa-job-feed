Five kinds of QA lead jobs

"QA lead" is not one job. The titles fall into five groups.

  • QA Lead (38 jobs). The classic role: one person owns quality for a product or a squad, plans testing, triages bugs and signs off releases. Examples are Quality Assurance Lead at Instinct (remote US) and Lead, Quality Assurance at Cardata (remote Canada).
  • Automation or SDET lead (23 jobs). These are engineering roles first. You design the framework, set coding standards for tests and wire them into CI. Kaluza, Avaya, Unanet and HighLevel all hire this way.
  • Test Lead (23 jobs). Common at consultancies, banks and government contractors. The work is closer to programme testing: test phases, UAT, defect reports and status for stakeholders. CACI, Leidos and HealthEdge are examples.
  • Lead QA engineer (21 jobs). A senior individual contributor who sets direction for other testers without formally managing them, as in Lead QA Engineer, Enterprise Platform at Keeper Security.
  • Team or chapter lead (10 jobs). These do include people management: one-to-ones, hiring and growth plans. JustPlay, Hostinger and Centrilogic use titles like "QA Chapter Lead" or "QA Team Lead".

From a senior role, the automation lead and lead QA engineer groups are the closest step. For the level above, see test manager jobs.

Three things stand out.

The lead still codes. 91 ads mention automation and 63 say "hands-on" in some form. Most companies want you to build or fix the framework yourself and then help others use it.

People skills are written into the job. 62 ads mention mentoring or coaching and 52 mention stakeholders. Expect interview questions about how you raised the level of a junior tester, or how you told a product owner that a release was not ready.

AI is already part of the role. 47 ads mention AI tools, LLMs or AI agents. Some roles are built around it: Endava hires a QA Automation Lead with AI agent testing experience, Hostinger an Agentic Quality Engineering Lead, and Codeminders a QA Automation Lead for API and MCP testing. Board-wide, our stricter tool tracker, which looks for AI or LLM testing as a skill, flags 125 job descriptions (11.4%). Lead ads more often mention AI as part of how the team works, not only as something to test.

How to get a QA lead job

  • Have about 5 years of testing experience. 64 ads state a minimum. The median is 6 years, and 5 years is the most common figure (21 ads).
  • Show a framework you own. Pick one project where you chose the tools, set up CI and got other people to write tests in it.
  • Show a person you helped. A short story about a tester you mentored, or a process you changed for the team, answers the 62 ads that ask for coaching.
  • Talk about releases, not test cases. Leads are judged on whether releases go out safely. Have numbers ready: escaped bugs, regression time, flaky test rate.
  • Learn one AI testing workflow. Use an AI tool to draft test cases or review a test suite, and know where it fails. 47 of 110 ads now ask about this.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a QA lead earn?

In the US, QA lead and test lead ads with a yearly range have a median midpoint of about $138,000, based on 20 ads. Across all QA job ads with pay, lead and manager titles have a median of $137,000. Canadian ranges in our sample run from CAD 100,000 to 160,000.

What does a QA lead do?

A QA lead owns testing for a product or team: test plans, automation, regression and the release decision. Most also coach other testers. In our sample, 91 of 110 ads mention automation and 62 mention mentoring.

What is the difference between a QA lead and a test manager?

A QA lead usually stays hands-on and leads a small team or one product. A test manager runs testing across a programme or several teams and spends more time on planning and reporting. Board-wide, we count 29 QA Lead and 28 Test Manager jobs today.

How many years of experience do you need to be a QA lead?

Most ads ask for 5 to 7 years. The median of the 64 ads that state a minimum is 6 years, and 5 years is the most common figure.

Are there remote QA lead jobs?

Yes, 101 of the 115 QA lead jobs in our sample are remote. Most are limited to one country, with the US (34) and India (14) the largest. Across the whole board, 90.7% of QA jobs are remote.
