Salaries: steady at $110,000

The median advertised salary across all QA jobs with a published yearly USD range is $110,000, based on 230 jobs. The middle half of those jobs pay between $82,000 (25th percentile) and $135,000 (75th percentile). The median is exactly where it was a week ago, so the extra jobs did not pull pay up or down.

Skills: Playwright and AI testing keep climbing

Selenium is still the most requested tool, named in 342 job descriptions (29.3%), but Playwright is close behind with 307 (26.3%) and added 32 mentions in a week, almost twice as many as Selenium's 17. SQL (282), Jira (272) and Postman (239) complete the top five.

The biggest relative change is AI and LLM testing. 154 QA jobs now mention testing AI features, LLM output or AI agents, up from 120 a week ago, a rise of about 28%. You can find them by searching for LLM testing jobs.

For programming languages, Python leads with 289 mentions (24.8%), followed by JavaScript (257), Java (231) and TypeScript (187). If you are choosing what to learn next, Playwright with TypeScript or Python covers the largest share of current automation roles. Where the jobs are

The United States has the most jobs that name a country, 403, up from 350 last week. India (106), Canada (65) and Poland (52) follow. Poland had the largest gain outside the US, from 39 to 52 jobs. Our guide to QA jobs in Poland explains which companies hire there, and you can see the current list on the Poland QA jobs page.

Remember that "remote" often still means one country. Of this week's 136 new remote listings, 51 were limited to the US and 9 to Poland, while only 12 were open worldwide and 9 to anywhere in Europe.

What to do with this week's data

  • Apply before the holidays. 293 jobs were added in the last seven days. Hiring often slows down in the second half of December, so applications sent now are more likely to get a reply this year.
  • Add one AI testing example to your CV. With 154 jobs asking for it, a short project where you test an LLM feature (prompt variations, output checks, guardrails) is an easy way to stand out.
  • Put Playwright next to Selenium. Selenium still has more mentions, but Playwright is growing faster. Listing both matches the two most requested tools on the board.
  • Check the location limit first. Read the "remote in" line before you apply, since many remote roles are restricted to one country.

Methodology

Board-wide figures (open jobs, companies, remote share, salaries, tools, languages and countries) come from QA Job Board data collected on 28 November 2026 and compared with the same data from 21 November 2026. Weekly listing counts and the ten jobs come from the public listings at qajobboard.com/jobs.json dated 21 to 27 November 2026; we picked one job per company and preferred posts with a published salary. Salary medians use the midpoint of each published yearly USD range, and seniority is based on words in the job title.
