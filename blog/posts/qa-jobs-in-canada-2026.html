Canada is one of the easiest places to find remote QA work right now, as long as you live there. On 21 November 2026 our public listings held 89 QA and software testing jobs tied to Canada, from 63 different employers. 75 of them are remote roles open only to people based in Canada, and the 21 ads that publish a yearly salary in Canadian dollars have a median midpoint of CAD 112,500. Most openings are mid-level or senior automation roles, and about one in seven is written in French.

If you want to skip straight to the listings, browse QA jobs in Canada. The page updates every day.

How big is the QA job market in Canada?

Bigger than most people expect. In today's board-wide count of 1,090 open QA jobs, Canada has 58 roles that list it as a location, which puts it third among all countries, behind the United States (350) and India (101), and ahead of Poland (39) and the United Kingdom (24). Our public listings keep jobs open a little longer than that daily snapshot, which is why the Canada page shows 89.

The employer base is wide. Those 89 jobs come from 63 companies, and only a few have more than two openings: Expleo (6, all in Montreal), nesto (5, plus 2 more under Nesto Group), Okta (4, in Toronto), Veeva Systems (3), PLATO (3) and NearSource Technologies (3).

Remote QA jobs in Canada: open to Canadian residents

The headline number is remote work. 75 of the 89 jobs are remote, and in every one of those 75 the location limit is Canada only. That matches the wider board, where 89.7% of all QA jobs are remote, but it also shows what "remote" usually means here: you need to live in Canada and be able to work there. Employers that hire remotely across the country include StackAdapt, Miratech, Smile Digital Health, Veeva Systems, Vena Solutions, Upgrade and Prenuvo.

The 14 office-based roles cluster in two cities. Montreal has Expleo's six consulting roles in payments testing, and Toronto has Okta's four Software Engineer in Test positions and a QA Analyst contract at Publicis Groupe.

If you are outside Canada, these jobs are mostly closed to you. Our remote QA jobs page includes roles open worldwide, and our guide to who is hiring remote QA engineers explains how location limits work.

How much do QA engineers earn in Canada?

Among the 21 Canada ads that publish a yearly salary in Canadian dollars, the median midpoint is CAD 112,500. The middle half of those ads sits between roughly CAD 86,000 and CAD 132,500. That is a small sample, so read it as a guide rather than a national average. Some real examples from the board:

  • Junior QA Engineer at 1VALET: CAD 50,000 to 60,000, the lowest band in the sample.
  • Platform Tester (Back-end Systems) at Intelerad: CAD 75,000 to 100,000, remote in Canada.
  • Senior Automation Tester at Smile Digital Health: CAD 120,000 to 135,000.
  • Senior Software Developer in Test (AI Portfolio) at Vena Solutions: CAD 114,750 to 155,250.
  • QA Architect at Cardata: CAD 130,000 to 160,000.

Two things stand out. First, Canadian employers publish pay more often than most: 30 of the 89 Canada jobs list a salary in some form, while only 17.9% of jobs across the whole board do. Second, the CAD median looks close to the board-wide median of $110,000, but that figure is in US dollars and is pulled up by US roles. Converted at our rough rate, CAD 112,500 is about USD 82,000. For a wider breakdown by role and seniority, see our QA engineer salary analysis.

Which QA roles are in demand in Canada?

Automation dominates. By category, the 89 jobs break down like this: QA Automation Engineer (22), QA Engineer (17), Software Development Engineer in Test (14), QA Analyst (12), Senior QA Engineer (11), QA Lead (5), Software Tester (3), and a handful of test manager, junior and test engineer roles.

Seniority skews up. 49 of the 89 titles include senior, lead, manager or architect, and only three are clearly entry-level: the 1VALET junior role, a QA Automation Engineer co-op at Flexspring and Lyft's hardware internship. If you are starting out, Canada is a hard market to enter remotely, and our guide to entry-level QA jobs covers where juniors have better odds.

SDET work is strong. Okta, Givecloud, DOZR, Aequilibrium, NearSource Technologies and Altinity are all hiring engineers in test, and 41 of the 89 ads mention SDET or "in test" somewhere in the title or description. Do you need French for QA jobs in Canada?

For most roles, no. But for jobs based in Quebec it often helps. 12 of the 89 titles are written in French or are bilingual, such as "Développeur(euse) senior en automatisation QA" at nesto or "Analyste qualité intermédiaire en automatisation" at PayFacto, and 16 ads mention French or bilingual skills somewhere in the description. Montreal-based employers like nesto, Expleo, PayFacto, itera, TEHORA and Frima Studio make up most of this group.

Which industries hire QA testers in Canada?

Fintech and lending stand out. nesto (mortgages), Upgrade (consumer lending), Cascade Debt, Aequilibrium (digital banking), Aquila Software Group (loan systems) and Expleo's payments testing practice account for a large share of the openings. Health technology is the next cluster, with Smile Digital Health, Intelerad, Prenuvo and Veeva Systems.

A few listings are not software testing. Pension calculation QA at TELUS, a native-speaker recording review contract and a hardware field quality internship match our QA filters but involve different work, so check the description before you apply.

Tips for applying to QA jobs in Canada

  • Confirm you meet the location rule. Almost every remote role requires you to live in Canada and hold the right to work there.
  • Lead with automation and APIs. API testing and CI/CD each appear in 52 of 89 ads, far more than any single framework.
  • Look at the employers behind the ads. Our best software companies to work for page ranks the companies hiring the most testers this month.

Methodology

We counted all open jobs in QA Job Board's public jobs feed on 21 November 2026 that are limited to candidates in Canada or list a Canadian office (89 listings), then searched their titles and full descriptions for tools, skills and languages. The salary median uses the 21 ads with a yearly range in Canadian dollars, excluding one duplicate, and takes the midpoint of each range. Board-wide figures (1,090 jobs, country counts, remote share, salary disclosure, tool shares and the USD salary median) come from our daily insights file built from the same sources.
