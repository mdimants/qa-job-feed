Based on 27 open QA jobs in Romania on QA Job Board, November 2026.

Romania has a small but steady QA job market, and almost all of it is remote. Right now QA Job Board lists 27 QA and testing jobs open to candidates in Romania, from 18 companies. 25 of them are remote roles limited to people living in Romania, and only 2 are on-site. Most openings are for automation engineers, Java is asked for far more often than on the rest of the board, and there are no junior roles at the moment. If you speak German, a few of the better-paid consulting roles become open to you.

You can see every open role on the QA jobs in Romania page, which updates every day.

How many QA jobs are there in Romania?

On 19 November 2026 we counted 27 open QA jobs that are located in Romania or open only to candidates in Romania. A few employers post the same role twice (Wavestone Consulting Romania and Codekeeper both have repeated listings), so the number of distinct openings is 24. 16 of the 27 listings were published in the last 30 days.

For context, our daily insights file, which tracks 1,119 QA jobs from 755 companies, puts Romania at 16 jobs. That places it close to Germany, Ukraine and Spain (18 each) and well behind Poland (41). If you are open to the wider region, read our guides to QA jobs in Poland and QA jobs in Germany, which cover two larger markets that also hire remotely.

Remote or on-site?

Romania follows the same pattern as the board as a whole. Of the 27 jobs, 25 are remote (93%) and 2 are on-site. Across all 1,119 jobs on the board, 91.8% are remote. The difference in Romania is that the remote roles are almost always tied to the country: 25 of them are limited to Romania only. The work is remote, but payroll and contracts are set up for one country.

If you live in Romania you can also apply for roles marked as open worldwide or across Europe. Those are not counted here. Browse them on the remote QA jobs page.

Three things stand out.

  • Java matters more here. Nearly half of the Romanian listings mention Java, compared with about one in five across the board. Banking and enterprise clients, which hire a lot through Romanian consultancies, still run large Java and Selenium test suites. You can find more of these roles with a search for Java QA jobs.
  • Playwright is less common than elsewhere. It appears in 22% of Romanian listings, compared with 25.5% board-wide, while Cypress (30%) is ahead of it. Our Playwright vs Selenium vs Cypress comparison shows the global trend, which Romania is following more slowly.
  • ISTQB is still asked for. 8 of the 27 descriptions mention it, usually as a "nice to have". Consulting firms that place testers with clients like a certificate they can show.

Mobile testing also comes up often. 11 descriptions mention mobile, and 3 roles have it in the title, including a Sr. Mobile QA Analyst at CrowdStrike and a Senior Test Automation Engineer for mobile at Software Mind.

Languages: English is enough, German helps

Not one of the 27 descriptions asks for Romanian. Most are written in English, and 15 mention English directly. German is the second language that matters: 6 descriptions mention German, all from Wavestone Consulting Romania and Sigma Software. These are Test Manager and senior automation roles for German-speaking clients. One performance testing role at Be | Shaping the Future asks for both English and French.

How much do QA jobs in Romania pay?

We can't give a reliable Romanian salary figure. Only one employer in this set published a range, and it was too wide ($10,000 to $100,000 a year) to tell you anything useful. Across the whole board only 18.1% of jobs show a salary.

For comparison, the board-wide median for jobs that do post pay is $110,000 a year (202 jobs), and QA automation engineers have a median of $114,000 (21 jobs). Most of those salaries come from US-based roles, so do not use them as a target for a Romania-only contract. They are more useful when you apply for worldwide remote roles that pay in USD. Our QA engineer salary report breaks pay down by role and seniority.

Contract type matters too. 24 of the 27 Romanian listings are full-time and 3 are contracts. If a role is offered as a contract or through a PFA, ask who pays for holidays and sick days before you compare it with a salaried offer.

How to get hired faster

  • Lead with Java and an automation framework. If your CV shows a Java and Selenium (or Cypress) project, you match about half of the market here.
  • Add a modern tool. Playwright is less common in Romania than elsewhere, and that will not last. One small Playwright project shows you are ready for the next round of hiring.
  • Mention German if you have it. Even B1 German opens up the consulting roles that tend to be more senior.
  • Apply early. With about 16 new listings a month, each role gets attention quickly. Check the Romania page a few times a week.
