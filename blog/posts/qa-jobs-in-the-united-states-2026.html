The United States is still the largest market for QA jobs, and almost all of the openings we track there are remote. We counted 558 software QA roles limited to candidates in the US, posted since 1 October 2026 by 385 different companies. Half of them publish a salary, and the median advertised midpoint is $116,650 a year. The catch: about one in six asks for US citizenship or a government clearance, and sponsorship is rare.

How many QA jobs are open in the United States?

In today's board-wide snapshot of 1,219 open QA jobs, 404 are tagged with the United States as a location. That is more than the next five countries combined: India (109), Canada (68), Poland (48), the Philippines (35) and Mexico (34).

For this article we pulled every public listing posted since 1 October 2026 whose location limit is the United States. That gave 558 jobs. The difference from 404 comes from the wider date window: the daily snapshot only counts jobs that are still open today.

A few things stand out in those 558 jobs:

  • All of them are remote within the US. Board-wide, 91.8% of our QA jobs are remote, and US roles follow the same pattern. You can apply from any state, although some ads mention time zone or occasional travel.
  • Most are full-time. 492 are full-time, 54 are contracts and 12 are part-time.
  • Demand is spread out. 385 companies posted these roles, and the biggest single employer accounts for 16 of them. No one company dominates.

Roles open to several countries, including the US, also appear on the remote QA jobs page. If you can work from Canada as well, see our guide to QA jobs in Canada.

How much do QA engineers earn in the US?

US employers publish pay far more often than the rest of the market. Across the whole board only 19.0% of QA jobs list a salary. Among the US-limited roles, 276 of 558 (49%) give an annual range in US dollars. Several states, including California, Colorado, New York and Washington, require pay ranges in job ads, which explains much of the gap.

Using the midpoint of each published range:

  • 25th percentile: $99,384
  • Median: $116,650
  • 75th percentile: $140,000

That is above the board-wide median of $111,000 (232 jobs with a salary in today's snapshot), which mixes US pay with lower ranges from other countries.

Pay by seniority

We also grouped US roles by words in the title (junior, senior, lead, manager and similar):

  • Junior: $75,000 median, but only 9 published salaries, so this is a weak signal
  • Mid-level (no seniority word): $110,000 from 131 salaries
  • Senior: $121,500 from 54 salaries
  • Lead, manager, staff or principal: $140,200 from 82 salaries

Junior roles are scarce. Only 29 of the 558 US jobs have a junior, entry-level or associate title. If you are starting out, read our guide to entry-level QA jobs before you apply.

Citizenship, clearance and visa sponsorship

This is the biggest difference between US QA jobs and the rest of the board. Many US openings come from government contractors, and they come with conditions:

  • 102 of 558 descriptions (18%) mention a security clearance or a public trust background check.
  • 88 (16%) mention US citizenship.
  • 54 say outright that the company will not sponsor a work visa. Only 4 say sponsorship is available.

The top employers confirm it. The companies with the most US QA postings in this period are CACI International (16), Keeper Security (9), Peraton (8), Bright Vision Technologies (8), ICF (7), General Dynamics Information Technology (6), Veeva Systems (6) and Steampunk (6). Several of these work mainly on federal contracts.

If you are a US citizen, holding or being able to obtain a clearance opens a large group of roles that many other testers cannot apply for. If you need sponsorship, filter early: search the description for "sponsor" before you spend time on a cover letter. Non-US candidates who want to work for US companies usually have better luck with nearshore roles; see QA jobs in Latin America for US companies.

How to get a remote QA job in the US

  1. Use the salary data in your search. With half of US ads listing pay, you can skip roles below your range before applying. Ask for a figure near the 75th percentile ($140,000) only if you have automation and CI experience to back it up.
  2. Read the eligibility lines first. Citizenship, clearance and "no sponsorship" lines are usually near the end of the description. Check them before anything else.
  3. Show code, not only test cases. QA automation engineers have a median about $30,000 higher than QA analysts in our sample. A small public repository with a Playwright or Selenium suite running in GitHub Actions helps you move into those roles.
  4. Apply soon after posting. In the last 7 days, 91 new US-limited QA roles were posted. Fresh listings get fewer applicants, so check the US page a few times a week.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a QA engineer make in the United States?

The median advertised salary for US-limited QA jobs on our board is $116,650 a year, based on 276 jobs that publish pay. The middle half of those ranges sits between about $99,000 and $140,000. Senior QA engineers have a median of $135,000.

Are there remote QA jobs in the US?

Yes. All 558 US-limited QA jobs we counted since 1 October 2026 are remote, which matches the 91.8% remote share across the whole board. Most require you to live and be authorized to work in the US.

Do US QA jobs sponsor work visas?

Rarely. Only 4 of 558 US QA ads say sponsorship is available, while 54 say the company will not sponsor. About 16% mention US citizenship and 18% mention a clearance or public trust check.

What skills do US QA job posts ask for most?

Selenium is mentioned in 24.4% of US QA ads, followed by Jira and Python at 22.4% each. SQL, Java and Playwright each appear in about 18%.

Which companies hire the most QA engineers in the US?

In our data, CACI International posted the most US QA roles (16), followed by Keeper Security (9), Peraton (8) and Bright Vision Technologies (8). Overall, 385 companies posted US-limited QA jobs since October.
