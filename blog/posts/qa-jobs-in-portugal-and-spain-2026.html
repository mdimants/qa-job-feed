Yes, you can find QA work in Portugal and Spain without speaking the local language, but the two countries are not the same. On 20 November 2026 we counted 33 open QA and testing jobs tied to Portugal (17) or Spain (16). In Portugal, 15 of the 17 ads are written in English. In Spain it is closer to half: 8 of 16 are in English, and the other 8 are written in Spanish and expect you to work in it. Almost all of these roles are remote within the country, and most are full-time employment rather than freelance contracts.

How big is the QA market in Portugal and Spain?

Small, but real. Portugal and Spain sit at 11th and 12th place among countries on the board, right next to each other. For comparison, Poland has 40 open QA jobs and Germany has 16, so Spain alone is now on par with Germany. If you are willing to look at both countries together, the 33 roles outnumber any single Western European country on the board, including the United Kingdom with 24.

The market is also fresh. 26 of the 33 jobs were posted in the last seven days, which matches the board as a whole, where 879 of 1,130 jobs are less than a week old. QA hiring in Iberia moves fast, so a role you see today may be gone in two weeks.

Portugal: the easier market for English speakers

Portugal has become a hub for international product companies that hire remote staff under Portuguese contracts. That shows in the ads. Almost every Portugal listing is written in English, and several companies hire for Portugal and Poland at the same time (payabl. and Fluxon both do), which tells you they treat it as an English-speaking engineering location.

Examples of what is open right now:

  • Senior QA Automation Engineer (Playwright) at Valtech, remote in Portugal.
  • Quality Engineer (Automation and AI) at Zyte, a web data company hiring remotely in Portugal.
  • QA Analyst at Sword Health, one of the few Portugal ads with a published salary.
  • QA Engineer (Banking) at payabl., a payments company that also has a database-focused QA role open.

Two of the 17 Portugal jobs are written in Portuguese, and one of those (a manual and automation tester role at Movilges) asks for Spanish as well. Local consultancies such as Caixa Mágica Software and Devoteam also hire testers, often for client projects in telecom or e-commerce.

Spain: half the jobs expect Spanish

Spain splits into two groups. The first is international companies writing in English: Agilent, Akamai, Airalo, DNA.inc and Scopely, the mobile games studio with two on-site roles in Barcelona, including a Senior Dev QA role on Yahtzee with Buddies. The second is Spanish consultancies and service firms writing in Spanish, such as TECDATA Engineering (four open roles), knowmad mood, Globe Testing, Initium Software and IRIUM.

The Spanish-language ads lean towards banking and payments. All four TECDATA roles involve payment systems, for example QA Tester (Medios de pago). If you speak Spanish and have tested card payments, core banking or mainframe-backed systems, this is the part of the market where you have an edge.

One caveat: two of the 16 Spain listings are language-recording quality checks for native speakers (one for Basque), not software testing. That leaves 14 software QA roles in Spain.

What do QA jobs in Portugal and Spain pay?

Salary data is thin here. Only a handful of the 33 ads publish a salary in euros, so treat the figures below as examples, not a market average:

  • QA Analyst at Sword Health (Portugal): EUR 24,150 to 37,950 a year.
  • QA Tester at TECDATA Engineering (Spain): EUR 28,000 a year.
  • QA Tester at IRIUM (Spain): EUR 35,000 to 37,000 a year.
  • Senior Quality Engineer at DNA.inc (Spain): EUR 62,400 a year.

These are well below the board-wide median of $110,000 for jobs that publish pay (205 ads), but that median is pulled up by US roles. Across the board only 18.1% of QA ads include a salary at all, and Iberian employers disclose even less. For a wider view of pay by role and seniority, see our QA engineer salary analysis.

Remote within Iberia vs remote from anywhere

31 of the 33 jobs are listed as remote, which is in line with the board's 90.9% remote share. But remote here almost always means "remote, if you live in Portugal" or "remote, if you live in Spain". 23 of the 31 remote roles are limited to those two countries only. A few open wider: Agilent hires in Spain, the UK and the US, Airalo in Spain and the UK, and Akamai in Czechia, Poland and Spain.

If you live elsewhere in Europe and want to work for these companies, look at the multi-country listings first, or check our remote QA jobs page and filter by the countries you can legally work from. Our guide to who is hiring remote QA engineers explains how location limits work in practice.

Tips for applying from abroad or as a newcomer

  • Check the residency requirement. Most employers here hire through a local contract and need you to be resident and allowed to work in the country.
  • Read the ad language as a signal. If the ad is in Spanish or Portuguese, the team almost certainly works in that language, even if English is listed as a plus.
  • Lead with automation if you can. In Portugal especially, Playwright, Selenium and API testing show up far more often than manual-only roles. If you are manual-only, Spain's functional testing roles in payments are the better fit, and our manual QA tester jobs page lists more.
  • Compare with Poland. Several Portugal employers also hire in Poland, and our QA jobs in Poland report covers that larger market.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a QA job in Portugal if I only speak English?

Yes, Portugal is one of the better European markets for English speakers. 15 of the 17 open QA jobs in Portugal on QA Job Board are written in English. Most are remote roles at international product companies.

Do QA jobs in Spain require Spanish?

About half do. Of the 16 open QA jobs in Spain, 8 are written in Spanish and expect you to work in it, mostly at consultancies in banking and payments. The other 8 are at international companies that write their ads in English.

How much does a QA engineer earn in Spain or Portugal?

Published salaries in our sample range from about EUR 24,000 for an analyst role to EUR 62,400 for a senior quality engineer. Very few Iberian ads list pay, so these are examples rather than an average. Across the whole board, the median advertised QA salary is $110,000, driven largely by US roles.

Are QA jobs in Portugal and Spain remote?

Mostly, yes: 31 of the 33 jobs are remote. But 23 of those are limited to people living in Portugal or Spain, so remote usually means remote within the country, not from anywhere.

Which test automation tools are most in demand in Portugal and Spain?

Selenium (12 of 33 jobs) and Playwright (11) lead, followed by Cypress, Postman and SQL. That is close to the board overall, where Selenium appears in 30.5% of job descriptions.

Where can I find QA jobs in Portugal and Spain?

QA Job Board has dedicated pages for both: QA jobs in Portugal and QA jobs in Spain. They are updated daily from company career pages and job sources.
