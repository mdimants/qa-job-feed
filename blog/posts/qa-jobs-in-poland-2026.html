How many QA jobs are open in Poland?

On 18 November 2026 the board had 58 open QA roles in Poland from 45 employers. Of those, 39 were posted in the last 30 days, so the list is mostly fresh rather than old listings that nobody has closed.

Poland also stands out in our daily feed. Of the 996 fresh QA jobs collected today, 40 are in Poland, which puts it in fourth place after the United States (307), India (86) and Canada (59), and ahead of every other European country. The United Kingdom has 19 and Germany has 15 in the same feed. For a country of its size, that is a lot of testing work.

The demand is spread out. The largest employer, Wavestone Poland, has five open roles, followed by CGI with four, and Future Processing and CodiLime with three each. The rest are one or two roles per company: software houses, banking and payments teams, US and Western European product companies hiring in Poland, and a few large names such as NVIDIA, InPost, JetBrains and Endava.

Seniority: a market for experienced testers

When we sort the titles by seniority, 29 of the 58 roles are senior, staff, lead or manager positions, 28 are mid-level, and only one is explicitly junior (a Junior QA Specialist role at MasterBorn). That matches the global picture: across the whole board, only 42 of 996 jobs in today's feed are classified as junior.

For a junior tester in Poland this means two things. First, apply to mid-level roles that list 1 to 2 years of experience, because few employers post a "junior" title at all. Second, show something concrete: a small automation project with Playwright or Cypress and a set of API tests in Postman gets you further than a certificate alone. Our junior QA roadmap covers how to build that.

How many QA jobs in Poland are remote?

Most of them. 50 of the 58 Polish roles (86%) are listed as remote, 7 are on-site and 1 is hybrid. The on-site roles are mainly at large IT service providers such as CGI.

A remote role in Poland usually means "remote within Poland", not "work from anywhere". Employers hire in Poland because of tax, contract and time-zone reasons, so you normally need to live there or have the right to work there. The board-wide figure is similar: 91.8% of the jobs in today's feed are remote, but most of them are limited to one country or region. If you can work from several countries, the remote QA jobs page shows every remote role, and our article on who hires remote QA engineers explains the location limits.

Contracts: B2B or employment contract?

18 of the 58 posts (31%) mention B2B, the Polish setup where you invoice the company as a self-employed contractor. Seven mention an employment contract (umowa o pracę), and several offer both. The feed also lists 7 roles as contract work and 50 as full-time, but in practice "full-time" in Poland can still be a full-time B2B engagement.

B2B usually pays a higher gross amount, but you cover your own taxes, social security and holidays.

Three differences from the global market are worth noting. Playwright is ahead of Selenium in Poland (31% against 24%), while board-wide Selenium is still the most mentioned tool. Polish teams that build new frameworks tend to pick Playwright, so it is the better tool to learn if you are choosing now: browse Playwright jobs.

Second, Jira, SQL and TestRail all appear above the global rate, which fits the many outsourcing and banking projects with formal test workflows.

Third, ISTQB is mentioned in about a quarter of posts. It is rarely a hard requirement, but it is listed as a plus more often than in US job ads. If you already have the Foundation Level, put it on your CV.

API testing is common too: 31 of the 58 descriptions mention APIs, and 16 mention mobile testing. If you want to compare tools in more detail, see Playwright vs Selenium vs Cypress.

Do you need to speak Polish?

Usually not. 35 of the 58 descriptions (60%) mention English, often as the working language, while only 9 (16%) mention Polish. A few roles ask for German instead, for example Wavestone's "Test Manager with German" and "Senior QA Automation Engineer with German", which serve German-speaking clients from Poland. If you speak German, those roles are worth a look, and so is our guide to QA jobs in Germany.
