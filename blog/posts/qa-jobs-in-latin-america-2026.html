Latin America is one of the steadiest sources of remote QA work on our board, and almost all of it is remote. On 26 November 2026 we found 175 QA and software testing jobs limited to candidates in Latin America, from 115 different employers, posted since 1 October. Every one of them is a remote role. Brazil has the most openings (53), followed by Mexico (36) and Colombia (19), and another 29 ads accept candidates anywhere in the region. Most are mid-level automation roles, and more than half ask for English.

If you want the listings first, browse QA jobs in Brazil or QA jobs in Mexico. Both pages update every day.

How many QA jobs are open in Latin America?

In today's board-wide snapshot of 1,219 open QA jobs, Mexico has 34 roles that list it as a location and Brazil has 32. That puts Mexico sixth among all countries, behind the United States (404), India (109), Canada (68), Poland (48) and the Philippines (35), and Brazil eighth, just behind the United Kingdom (33). Colombia adds 18. Our public listings keep jobs a little longer than that daily snapshot, which is why the counts below are higher.

To get the regional picture, we took every job on the board whose location limit is a Latin American country or "LATAM", kept roles posted since 1 October 2026, and removed a handful of ads that were not software testing (automotive supplier quality, legal intake and document formatting). Who hires remote QA engineers in Latin America?

Two kinds of employers dominate. The first is nearshore software companies and staffing firms that place Latin American engineers on projects for clients in the United States. In this group we see CI&T (8 jobs), XKTalent (7), Sequoia Connect (6), Nagarro (4), Software Mind (4), Xideral (4), CRH Talento de IT (4), Alcor (3) and Dev.Pro (3). The second is product companies that hire directly in the region, such as Airbnb, Upgrade, Onfleet, Motorola Solutions, Loadsmart, Fortive, Popmenu and Tether.

Each country has its own pattern:

  • Brazil has the widest employer base: 53 jobs from 35 companies. Local firms such as CI&T, Quality Digital, FCamara and Compass UOL post many roles in Portuguese, and 20 of the 53 Brazil titles are written in Portuguese ("Analista de QA Pleno", "Engenheiro de Qualidade"). See QA jobs in Brazil.
  • Mexico is more concentrated: 36 jobs from 19 companies, led by staffing and consulting firms (Sequoia Connect, Xideral, CRH Talento de IT, Nagarro, Alcor). Many of these are placements on US client teams. See QA jobs in Mexico.
  • Colombia is spread thin: 19 jobs from 17 companies, including Softtek, Applaudo, CaseWare, Jobsity and Nagarro. See QA jobs in Colombia.
  • Argentina has 9 jobs from 9 different companies, among them Tether, Allata, Azumo, Ryz Labs and Lifted. That is a small sample. See QA jobs in Argentina.

Do you need English for these jobs?

For most of them, yes. 97 of the 175 ads (55%) mention English, and 49 ask for an advanced, fluent, B2 or C1 level. That reflects who the work is for: when a nearshore firm places you on a US client team, you join their stand-ups, write bug reports in English and talk to their product managers. Ads in Portuguese or Spanish for local companies are the main exception.

Time zones are the other half of the deal. Latin America overlaps with US working hours far better than India or Eastern Europe, which is why US companies hire here. Several ads spell this out, asking you to work US Eastern or Pacific hours or to overlap with a US team. Expect that, and check the hours before you apply.

What QA roles and skills are in demand?

Automation leads. By category, the 175 jobs include QA Engineer (50), QA Automation Engineer (38), QA Analyst (15), Senior QA Engineer (12), SDET (9), manual testers (8) and software testers (6). Seniority sits in the middle: 99 titles read as mid-level, 66 as senior, lead or specialist, and only 10 as junior. How much do QA engineers in Latin America earn?

Few ads say. Only 7 of the 175 jobs publish a salary, and they mix currencies and pay periods, so we cannot give a reliable regional median. Across the whole board, 19.0% of jobs disclose pay and the median midpoint is $111,000, but that figure is driven by US roles and does not apply to nearshore contracts. Some real ranges from the region:

  • Senior SDET, Agentic Quality at Onfleet: USD 70,000 to 95,000 a year, open across Latin America.
  • Senior QA Automation at CI&T: BRL 10,000 to 13,000, which in Brazil reads as a monthly salary.
  • QA Automation Engineer (Playwright / AI-Enabled Workflows) at TalentCross: USD 3,500, most likely per month.

Contracts are common. 37 of the 175 jobs (21%) are listed as contract roles, and 23 ads mention pay in US dollars. Many nearshore firms hire you as an independent contractor paid in USD rather than as a local employee. That can mean higher pay than local salaries, but also no local benefits, so compare the full package. Our QA engineer salary analysis has the board-wide breakdown by role and seniority.

How to get hired by a US company from Latin America

  1. Write your CV and LinkedIn in English. Most of these roles are screened by English-speaking recruiters, and 49 ads set a minimum English level.
  2. Show automation, not just test cases. A public repository with a Playwright or Cypress suite running in GitHub Actions proves the two skills that appear most often here: a modern framework and CI/CD.
  3. Apply to region-wide roles too. 29 jobs accept anyone in Latin America. They are easy to miss if you only check your country page.
  4. Ask about the contract. Before you accept, find out whether you are an employee or a contractor, which currency you are paid in, and which hours you must cover.

Frequently asked questions

Are there remote QA jobs in Latin America?

Yes, and nearly all QA jobs for the region are remote. We counted 175 open QA roles limited to Latin American candidates, every one of them remote. Across the whole board, 91.8% of QA jobs are remote.

Which Latin American country has the most QA jobs?

Brazil has the most, with 53 open QA roles from 35 companies in our listings. Mexico follows with 36 and Colombia with 19. In today's board-wide snapshot Mexico has 34 jobs and Brazil 32.

Do I need English to work in QA for a US company from Latin America?

In most cases, yes. 97 of the 175 Latin America ads mention English, and 49 ask for an advanced or fluent level. Roles at local Brazilian or Mexican companies are often posted in Portuguese or Spanish and may not need it.

How much does a remote QA engineer in Latin America earn?

There is not enough public data for a reliable median, since only 7 of the 175 ads publish pay. Examples range from about USD 3,500 a month on a contract to USD 70,000 to 95,000 a year for a senior SDET. Board-wide, the median advertised QA salary is $111,000, mostly from US roles.

Which testing tools should I learn for Latin American QA jobs?

Playwright and Selenium are the most requested, in 74 and 71 of the 175 ads. Postman, SQL and Cypress follow, and CI/CD appears in 88 ads. JavaScript is the most requested programming language in the region.

Where can I find QA jobs in Latin America?

Start with our country pages for Brazil, Mexico, Colombia and Argentina, which update every day. Region-wide roles also appear on the remote QA jobs page.

Methodology

Salary examples are quoted as published.
