What counts as a quality assurance job?

The phrase "quality assurance" covers two different professions, and this matters when you search for it. In software, QA means testing applications: writing test cases, finding bugs, building automated test suites and deciding whether a release is ready. In pharma, manufacturing, call centres and finance, QA means checking that processes follow standards and regulations.

The two mix on job sites. On our live board, 224 listings have "Quality Assurance" in the title. When we checked those titles for words such as engineer, analyst, tester, test, automation or software, 111 matched. The other 113 were titles such as "Quality Assurance Associate", "Manager, Quality Assurance & Regulatory Affairs" or "Content Quality Assurance". Some of those are still software roles, but many are not. If you want to test software, search for the role name (QA engineer, QA analyst, software tester, SDET) rather than "quality assurance" alone, and read the first paragraph of the description before you apply.

QA Engineer

This is the general QA role and the largest group, with 295 jobs. A QA engineer usually owns testing for one product team: test planning, exploratory testing, API checks and some automation. Most descriptions expect you to write at least basic automated tests. You can see them all on the QA Engineer jobs page.

QA Automation Engineer and SDET

Together these make up 262 jobs, almost a quarter of the feed. The work is closer to software development: building a test framework, running tests in CI and fixing flaky suites. QA Analyst

QA analyst is the title that sits closest to the classic meaning of quality assurance. The work leans on requirements, test cases, regression and user acceptance testing, often in banking, insurance, healthcare and government projects. On the live board there are 146 QA analyst listings from 112 companies, and 109 of them are remote. Of the 39 that posted a yearly salary in US dollars, the median midpoint is $87,500. How much do quality assurance jobs pay?

Across the 202 jobs that showed a salary, the median is $110,000 a year. The middle half of salaries falls between $83,000 and $135,000. Only 18.1% of QA jobs in the feed publish a salary at all, and most of those are US roles, where pay transparency laws apply in several states. Jobs in Europe, India and Latin America post salaries less often, so they are under-represented in these numbers, and pay there is usually lower than in the US.

Seniority changes pay more than job title:

  • Junior: median $85,000 (15 salaries)
  • Mid-level: median $104,000 (91 salaries)
  • Senior: median $115,000 (42 salaries)
  • Lead / Manager: median $139,000 (54 salaries)

For a full breakdown by role and level, see our QA engineer salary report.

How many QA jobs are for juniors?

Very few. Of the 1,119 jobs, 650 (58%) are mid-level, 243 (22%) are senior and 177 (16%) are lead or manager roles. Only 49 (4%) are junior. There are also 9 internships in the feed.

This does not mean you cannot start in QA. It means most companies hire testers with one to three years of experience, and you compete with more people for each junior opening. Where are quality assurance jobs located?

The United States has the most QA jobs, with 352 in today's feed, or 31% of the total. It is followed by India (104), Canada (62), Poland (41), the Philippines (32), Brazil (27), Mexico (26) and the United Kingdom (24). In Europe, Germany, Ukraine and Spain have 18 each.

Most of these jobs are remote, but remote rarely means "from anywhere". A remote job is usually limited to one country or region because of tax, payroll and time zones. 705 of the 755 hiring companies have at least one remote QA role, so the useful filter is your own country. Start with the remote QA jobs list and check the location line on each post.

Contract work is common too. 171 jobs (15%) are contracts, 920 (82%) are full-time, and the rest are part-time, temporary or internships.

What skills do QA jobs ask for?

We searched 1,082 job descriptions for tools and languages. The most requested are:

  1. Selenium, in 29.5% of descriptions
  2. Playwright, 25.5%
  3. SQL, 24.8%
  4. Jira, 23.1%
  5. Postman, 21.4%
  6. Cypress, 17.8%

Python is the most requested language (26.1%), followed by JavaScript (20.9%), Java (19.8%) and TypeScript (15.8%). Testing of AI and LLM features already appears in 12.2% of descriptions, more than JMeter or Docker.

A practical set for most QA jobs today is SQL, API testing with Postman, one UI automation tool (Playwright or Selenium) and one language. Python is a good default. If you want to see which companies hire for these skills, search for Playwright jobs.

How to find a quality assurance job faster

  • Search by role, not by "quality assurance". You avoid pharma and operations QA listings and get software testing roles.
  • Apply in the first week. 272 of the jobs in the feed were posted in the last seven days. Fresh posts get fewer applications.
  • Filter by your country. Most remote jobs have a location limit, so a filter saves you time.
  • Show automation, even in a manual role. One small Playwright or Selenium project on GitHub puts you ahead of many applicants.

Frequently asked questions

What does a quality assurance engineer do?

A QA engineer tests software to find bugs before users do, by writing test cases, testing by hand and building automated tests. QA Engineer is the most common QA title on our board, with 295 of 1,119 open jobs.

How much do quality assurance jobs pay?

The median advertised salary for QA jobs is $110,000 a year, based on 202 job posts that showed pay. The middle half of salaries is between $83,000 and $135,000, and most of these posts are in the United States.

Are quality assurance jobs remote?

Yes, most are. 91.8% of the QA jobs in our feed are remote, but many are limited to candidates in one country or region.

Is it hard to get a quality assurance job with no experience?

It is harder than it was. Only 49 of 1,119 open QA jobs (4%) are junior roles, so you need a small portfolio and some automation skills to stand out.

What skills do I need for a QA job?

Most QA jobs ask for SQL, API testing and one automation tool. Selenium appears in 29.5% of job descriptions, Playwright in 25.5% and SQL in 24.8%.

Where can I find quality assurance jobs?

Methodology

Board-wide figures (1,119 jobs, 755 companies, roles, seniority, countries, tools and salary medians) come from our daily insights file for 19 November 2026, built from the fresh jobs collected that day. Salary medians use the midpoint of each posted range in US dollars a year.
