Yes, remote SDET jobs are easy to find right now. Nine out of ten SDET (Software Development Engineer in Test) posts we track are remote, they come from dozens of different companies, and they pay more than almost any other QA role, with a median advertised salary of $130,000 a year. The catch is that most of them want senior engineers who write production-quality code, and many limit which country you can work from. Below is who is hiring, where, for what pay, and what the job posts ask for.

How many SDET jobs are open?

Out of 996 open QA jobs on QA Job Board today, 62 are in the SDET category. That makes SDET the sixth largest QA job type, after QA Engineer (237), QA Automation Engineer (187), Senior QA Engineer (120), Software Tester (110) and QA Analyst (81). It is a smaller market than general QA, but it is a better paid one, and it overlaps a lot with automation roles. If you are an SDET, you should also look at QA automation engineer jobs, because many companies use the two titles for the same work.

We count a job as SDET when the title says SDET, "Software Engineer in Test" or "Software Developer in Test". You can see the current list on our SDET jobs page.

Seniority: most SDET jobs are senior

32 of the 59 SDET titles (54%) contain Senior, Sr., Lead, Staff or Principal, and a few more are managers or a director. Only two posts are clearly entry-level: an SDET I role at Modivcare and an on-site SDET intern role at Epic Games. Across all QA jobs, junior roles are rare too (42 of 996), so this is not unique to SDET, but it is stronger here. Companies hire an SDET to build and own a test framework, and they want someone who has done it before. How to get a remote SDET job

  1. Show code, not only tests. Put a framework on GitHub that someone else could use: Playwright with TypeScript or Python, fixtures, API setup, test data handling and a clear README.
  2. Run it in CI. Add a GitHub Actions workflow that runs the suite in Docker on every pull request and publishes a report. More than half of SDET posts mention GitHub Actions.
  3. Add one non-UI skill. An API test suite or a small k6 load test shows you can test below the browser.
  4. Apply where you are allowed to work. Check the location limit before you apply. Most remote SDET posts are limited to one country or region.
  5. Prepare for coding interviews. SDET interviews often include a live coding task as well as test design. Our QA automation interview questions cover the testing side.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a remote SDET earn?

The median advertised SDET salary on QA Job Board is $130,000 a year, based on 15 posts that publish pay. The middle half of salaries ranges from $89,000 to $185,000. Most of these salaries come from US companies.

Are most SDET jobs remote?

Yes. 91.5% of the SDET posts in our job file are remote. Most still require you to live in a specific country, most often the United States, India or Canada.

Is SDET better paid than QA engineer?

Yes, based on job posts. The SDET median is $130,000, compared with $98,000 for QA Engineer posts and $110,000 for all QA jobs. The SDET sample is small (15 salaries), so treat it as a range.

What programming language should an SDET learn?

Python and TypeScript are the most common. Python appears in 58% of SDET posts and TypeScript in 56%, with Java at 36%. Pick the language your target companies use for their product code.

Can a junior get an SDET job?

It is possible but rare. Only 2 of 59 SDET posts are clearly entry-level, while 32 are senior, lead, staff or principal roles. Most people move into SDET work from a QA automation role.

Where can I find remote SDET jobs?

QA Job Board lists 62 open SDET jobs, updated every day, most of them remote. Browse them on the SDET jobs page or search all remote QA jobs.

Methodology

Totals, categories, countries, salaries and the all-QA tool shares come from QA Job Board's daily data for 18 November 2026 (996 open QA jobs, 178 with a salary converted to USD per year). The SDET breakdowns (remote share, locations, companies, seniority and the SDET tool shares) come from the same-day job file, which contains 59 SDET posts; we counted keywords in titles and descriptions, so a post that mentions a tool only as "nice to have" still counts.
