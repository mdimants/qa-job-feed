Yes, companies still hire manual QA testers in 2026, but it is a small and specific market. Our daily data tags 39 of 1,130 QA jobs, about 3.5%, as "QA Manual Tester", and the live site lists 49 manual testing jobs when you include listings that are a few weeks old. Almost all of them are remote. The ones that pay best expect more than clicking through test cases: they want API checks with Postman, some SQL, and a habit of writing bug reports that developers can act on the first time.

Where are manual QA jobs?

42 of the 49 manual testing jobs are remote, and the other 7 are on-site. That is close to the board-wide figure: 90.9% of all QA jobs on QA Job Board are remote. Remote rarely means "from anywhere", though. Most listings limit applicants to one country:

  • United States: 6 remote listings, including Labelmaster, PerformYard, Payall, Lifted (an Upwork company) and Nagarro.
  • Poland: 5 remote listings from companies such as SOFTSWISS, Storware, Alter Solutions and ART2HIRE. See all QA jobs in Poland.
  • United Kingdom: 3 remote listings, two of them at InvestEngine, plus on-site roles at CGI.
  • Portugal and Ukraine: 3 each.
  • Latin America: Brazil, Mexico, Argentina and Peru together have 8 listings, several of them in Spanish or Portuguese.

Only 2 listings are open to applicants worldwide. If you live outside the US and Europe, filter by your country first; the remote QA jobs page lets you see what is open right now.

One company stands out: Flosum, a Salesforce DevOps vendor, has 9 open listings for the same "Manual QA Engineer" role in 9 countries, from Serbia and Romania to the US and Belgium. That single hiring push explains why Salesforce appears in 18% of manual testing descriptions. If you know Salesforce, browse Salesforce QA jobs.

Is automation required for manual QA jobs?

Not usually, but it helps. 24 of the 49 descriptions (49%) mention automation in some way. Selenium appears in 18 of them, Playwright in 14 and Cypress in 13. Reading the descriptions, most of these mentions are "nice to have" or describe working next to an automation team, for example by deciding which regression cases should be automated first. A few roles, such as "Tester Manual e Automação", openly combine both. If you want to know which tool to learn first, our article on the most in-demand QA automation tools ranks them by job count.

How much experience do manual QA jobs want?

Manual testing is often seen as an entry-level job, but the listings say otherwise. 13 of the 49 titles say Senior, Lead or Principal, 3 say Middle, and none say Junior. Of the 35 descriptions that state a number of years, the most common requirement is 2 years (13 jobs), followed by 3 years (9 jobs). 13 jobs ask for 4 years or more.

So manual testing is not an easy way in anymore. If you are starting from zero, our guide to QA jobs for freshers lists the entry-level roles that are actually open, and junior QA engineer jobs are tracked on their own page.

How much do manual QA testers earn?

We cannot give a reliable salary for manual testing jobs alone. Only 2 of the 49 manual listings publish a salary, which is far too few to calculate anything. Across the whole board, only 18.1% of QA jobs disclose pay.

The closest groups with enough data are these, all from jobs that publish a yearly salary in or converted to US dollars:

  • QA Analyst: median $91,000 (21 jobs with salary).
  • Software Tester: median $100,000 (19 jobs with salary).
  • QA Automation Engineer: median $115,000 (26 jobs with salary).

The groups are small, and most salaried jobs on the board are US-based, so treat these as rough US figures. The gap of about $15,000 to $24,000 between analyst or tester roles and automation roles is the clearest sign of what learning automation is worth. Which industries hire manual testers?

The manual listings come from 31 different companies. A few patterns are clear from the titles and descriptions:

  • Gaming and iGaming. Playson, Torrero (Casino Team) and SOFTSWISS all hire manual QA engineers. Games and casino products have a lot of visual and gameplay behaviour that is hard to automate.

  • Insurance and health. CoverGo has two manual roles, and Analyte Health has a Senior QA Analyst (Manual Tester) role.

How to get a manual QA job in 2026

  1. Learn Postman and basic SQL. They appear in 61% and 49% of manual testing jobs. A weekend with a public API and a sample database is enough to talk about them in an interview.
  2. Show your test cases and bug reports. 65% of listings mention writing test cases. Put two or three real examples in a portfolio, ideally from a public app you tested yourself.
  3. Know Jira and one test management tool. TestRail is the most common, in a third of the listings.
  4. Apply in your own country first. Most remote manual jobs are limited to one country, so a remote job "in Poland" is not open to someone in India.
  5. Read one automation tool, even if you do not use it daily. Half of the listings mention automation, and it is the clearest route to a higher salary later.

Frequently asked questions

Is manual testing still in demand in 2026?

Yes, but it is a small share of the QA market. 39 of the 1,130 QA jobs in our daily data, about 3.5%, are tagged as manual tester roles. Many software tester and QA analyst jobs are also mostly manual, so the real number of hands-on testing jobs is higher.

How much does a manual QA tester earn?

Too few manual testing jobs publish pay to give a precise number. The closest groups are QA Analyst jobs, with a median advertised salary of $91,000, and Software Tester jobs, with a median of $100,000. Both are based on small samples of mostly US jobs.

Can I get a remote manual QA job?

Yes. 42 of the 49 manual testing listings on QA Job Board are remote. Most are limited to one country, though, so check the location requirement before you apply.

Do manual testers need to know automation?

Usually not, but it helps. About half of manual testing descriptions mention automation, mostly as a nice-to-have. Postman, Jira and SQL are asked for more often than any automation tool.

Is ISTQB required for manual QA jobs?

No. Only 4 of the 49 manual testing listings mention ISTQB. Employers care more about examples of test cases and bug reports.

Where can I find manual QA tester jobs?

Methodology

Board-wide figures (job counts by role, remote share, tool shares and salary medians) come from QA Job Board's daily insights file for November 20, 2026, which covers 1,130 current QA jobs. The manual testing analysis uses the 49 jobs tagged "QA Manual Tester" in the site's public jobs feed on the same day; we searched their full descriptions for each skill and counted the years of experience stated. Salary medians include only jobs that publish a yearly salary.
