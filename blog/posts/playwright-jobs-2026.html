Playwright is now the second most requested testing tool in QA job posts, just behind Selenium, and it is the most requested tool in automation and SDET roles. About one in four QA jobs we track mentions Playwright. Most of those jobs are remote, most are mid-level or senior, and the advertised pay sits close to the QA market median of $110,000 a year. Below is who hires Playwright testers, what else they ask for, and what they pay.

Out of 1,004 QA job posts with a full description on QA Job Board, 262 (26.1%) mention Playwright. Only Selenium is mentioned more often, in 305 posts (30.4%). Cypress follows with 186 posts (18.5%).

Which roles ask for Playwright?

We counted the job categories among Playwright posts (267 posts in the same-day job file, see Methodology):

  • QA Automation Engineer: 93 posts (34.8%)
  • QA Engineer: 72 posts (27.0%)
  • Senior QA Engineer: 38 posts (14.2%)
  • SDET: 28 posts (10.5%)
  • QA Manual Tester: 14 posts (5.2%)

The manual tester line is worth a second look. Fourteen posts in the manual tester category still list Playwright, usually as a "nice to have" or as a path to automation inside the team. Knowing the basics of Playwright can help a manual tester stand out even when the job is mostly exploratory testing.

If you already write automated tests, the most direct places to look are QA automation engineer jobs and SDET jobs.

Seniority: Playwright jobs lean mid-level and senior

Based on job titles, 148 Playwright posts (55.4%) are mid-level, 83 (31.1%) are senior and 33 (12.4%) are lead or manager roles. Only 3 posts are junior. That is a very small group, so do not read too much into it, but the message is clear: companies that pick Playwright usually expect someone who can build or extend a test framework, not only run existing tests. Junior candidates are better off using Playwright in portfolio projects and applying to junior QA roles, as we explain in our junior QA roadmap.

Which programming language?

Playwright supports JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, Java and .NET, and the job posts reflect that mix:

  • JavaScript: 41.6% of Playwright posts
  • TypeScript: 39.7%
  • Python: 36.0%
  • Java: 26.6%
  • C# / .NET: 12.0%

If you are choosing a language to learn Playwright in, TypeScript is the safest choice: most Playwright examples and documentation use it, and JavaScript posts accept it. Python is a strong second, especially in companies where developers already write Python.

How to get a Playwright job

  1. Build one complete project. A small Playwright suite for a public demo site, with page objects or fixtures, API setup calls, and a GitHub Actions workflow that runs on every push. Put the link at the top of your CV.
  2. Show you can debug. Interviewers often ask about flaky tests, traces and retries. Know how to use the trace viewer and explain why auto-waiting removes most fixed sleeps.
  3. Add API testing. With Postman in 41.9% of Playwright posts, show that you can test APIs too, either with Playwright's request context or a separate tool.
  4. Keep Selenium on your CV if you have it. Since 61.0% of Playwright posts also mention Selenium, migration experience is a selling point.
  5. Prepare for the framework questions. Our list of QA automation interview questions covers Playwright vs Selenium, waits and locators.

Frequently asked questions

Is Playwright in demand in 2026?

Yes. Playwright is mentioned in 26.1% of QA job posts on QA Job Board, second only to Selenium. In QA Automation Engineer and SDET posts it is the most requested framework, mentioned in 52.6% of them.

How much does a Playwright tester earn?

QA posts that mention Playwright and publish a salary have a median of $109,000 a year, based on 36 salaries. Pay depends more on the role than on the tool: SDET posts have a median of $130,000.

Is Playwright better than Selenium for getting a job?

For automation roles, Playwright now appears more often: 52.6% of automation and SDET posts versus 43.9% for Selenium. Selenium still leads across all QA jobs, and 61.0% of Playwright posts mention Selenium as well, so knowing both is the strongest position.

Which language should I learn Playwright with?

TypeScript or JavaScript is the most common choice, with JavaScript in 41.6% and TypeScript in 39.7% of Playwright posts. Python is close behind at 36.0%, so it is also a good option.

Are Playwright jobs remote?

Most of them are. About 95% of Playwright posts on QA Job Board are remote, slightly more than the 91.5% share across all QA jobs, although many limit applicants to certain countries.

Methodology

We analysed 1,035 open QA jobs collected by QA Job Board on 16 November 2026; tool shares are based on the 1,004 posts with a full description. Role, seniority, country, co-skill and salary breakdowns for Playwright, Selenium and Cypress come from the same day's job file (267 Playwright posts), using the same keyword matching. Salaries were converted to annual USD, and medians are only shown for groups with at least 5 salaries.
