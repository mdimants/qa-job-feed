  • Internships and trainee roles (13). SDET Intern at Epic Games, Intern Game Tester at Ubisoft, Software Test Engineer Intern at CACI and a 6-month QA Engineer Intern role at Shift Technology. Many are tied to a season such as "Spring 2027" or "Summer 2027", so check the start date.

What skills do entry-level QA jobs ask for?

We read the descriptions of all 56 entry-level jobs and counted how often common skills appear. Automation is mentioned in 48% of them and manual testing in 45%, so most employers want both, or at least want you to understand both. The most common specific skills are:

  • SQL: 38% of entry-level descriptions
  • API testing (the word "API"): 32%
  • Python: 30%
  • Selenium and Jira: 23% each
  • Agile: 25%
  • Postman and Git: 18% each
  • Playwright: 16%
  • ISTQB: 9%

For comparison, across all QA jobs Selenium appears in 30.5% of descriptions and Playwright in 25.8%. Junior roles ask for frameworks less often and lean more on fundamentals: writing clear test cases and bug reports, checking data with SQL and testing an API with Postman. ISTQB is mentioned in fewer than one in ten entry-level jobs, so it helps, but it will not replace a project you can show.

How to get one of these jobs

The pattern in these 56 listings points to a simple plan:

  1. Build one small, public test project. Pick a demo web app, write 20 to 30 clear test cases, file five good bug reports and automate a few checks in Playwright or Selenium. Put it on GitHub with a short README.
  2. Learn SQL and API testing before a second framework. SQL and API testing appear in more entry-level descriptions than any single automation tool.
  3. Apply to remote roles early. Many get dozens of applicants in the first days. Listings on QA Job Board show the date they were posted.
  4. Read the title carefully. "Associate" can mean entry level at one company and senior at another. And be careful with "training and placement" listings: some programs charge you a fee before they place you. A real employer does not ask you to pay to get hired.
  5. Look past the word "junior". Many QA Analyst and Software Tester roles accept one year of experience or none. Browse QA analyst jobs and manual QA tester jobs too.

Frequently asked questions

Are there QA jobs for freshers?

Yes, but they are a small share of the market. Only 47 of the 1,130 QA jobs on QA Job Board are tagged junior, and we found 56 genuine entry-level software testing roles across 48 companies. Internships and graduate programs at larger companies make up a good part of them.

How much does a junior QA engineer earn?

The median advertised salary for junior QA jobs on our board is $85,000 a year, based on only 11 listings that publish a salary. Most of those are US roles, so pay in Europe, Latin America and Asia is usually lower.

Can I get a QA job without experience?

Yes, if you can show testing skills in another way. Employers in our data most often ask juniors for SQL, API testing and basic Python or Selenium, and a small public test project is the easiest way to prove those. Only about one in ten entry-level listings mentions ISTQB.

Are entry-level QA jobs remote?

Most of them are. 40 of the 56 entry-level QA jobs we found are remote, compared with 90.9% remote across all QA jobs on the board. Internships at large companies are more often onsite.

Where can I find QA jobs for freshers?

QA Job Board lists junior, intern and graduate QA roles on the junior QA engineer jobs page, updated every day. You can also search for QA internships or filter remote QA jobs.

Methodology

Board totals, seniority counts, salaries and board-wide tool shares come from QA Job Board's daily insights file generated on November 20, 2026. The entry-level count comes from all live listings in our public jobs feed on the same day: we matched titles containing junior, jr, entry level, graduate, fresher, intern, trainee, apprentice or associate, then removed senior roles (such as Associate Director), requisition codes and non-software QA roles by hand. Skill shares are the percentage of those 56 job descriptions that mention each term.
