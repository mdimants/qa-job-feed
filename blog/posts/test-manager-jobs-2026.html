Test manager jobs are a small but well-paid part of the QA market. On 22 November 2026 we found 34 software test manager, QA manager and QA director openings posted in the last 90 days, from 32 employers. 29 of them are remote, although almost all of those are limited to one country or region. The six US ads with a yearly salary range have a median midpoint of about $164,000. Most employers want someone who sets the test strategy and moves the team towards automation, not someone who only tracks test cases.

Three kinds of test manager jobs

The title "test manager" covers different work, and the ads split into three groups.

  • Test Manager (16 jobs). These run testing for a project or programme: test plans, test phases, defect management, UAT and the go/no-go decision.
  • QA Manager (9 jobs). These manage a QA team inside a product company and usually stay hands-on with automation. Examples are the QA Manager role at Knotch and the QA Manager for EPIC Loan Systems at Aquila Software Group, both remote in Canada.
  • Director or Head of QA (9 jobs). These own quality across an engineering organisation and often manage other managers. Examples include Priority, HHAeXchange, Sword Health, Netomi, Muse Group and Zayo Group.

If you are one step below this level, the QA lead jobs page has more openings, and lead roles are the usual way into test management.

Enterprise programmes: SAP, Workday, Oracle and Salesforce

A large share of test manager work is about big business systems. 6 of the 34 jobs name a platform in the title: three SAP roles (at Natuvion, Trackmind and FFI Advisory), a Workday Test Manager at Groove Global, an Oracle Fusion Test Manager at DXC Technology and a Salesforce Test Manager at Endava in Mexico. SAP is mentioned in 7 descriptions in total, and in 5 of them it is the system being tested, including CACI's SAP-based supply chain work and TAP's SAP S/4HANA Utilities programme in Germany.

These roles ask for different skills from product QA. Tricentis Tosca appears in 5 ads and ALM-style test management tools (HP ALM, OpenText, SAP Cloud ALM) in 4. UAT or user acceptance testing appears in 9. You can search SAP testing jobs to see the wider set, including tester roles.

How much do test managers earn?

Six US ads in our sample publish a yearly salary range. Their midpoints run from about $140,000 (CACI's Test Manager, $90,300 to $189,600) to $262,000 (Priority's Director of Engineering, Quality Assurance, $233,000 to $291,000), and the median midpoint is about $164,000. That is a small sample, so treat it as a guide. Other examples:

  • Test Manager, Professional Services at PTC: $132,000 to $198,000, remote in the US.
  • Software Test Manager at Agilent Technologies: $127,680 to $199,500.
  • Outside the US: Knotch pays CAD 136,000 to 163,000, Aquila CAD 130,000 to 150,000, Sword Health in Portugal €64,400 to €101,200, and TAP in Germany €70,000 to €90,000.

Our board-wide data shows the same pattern. Across all QA jobs with a published salary, the median is $111,000 a year, and for lead and manager titles it is $136,000 (54 ads). Senior QA engineers sit at $128,000. Only 18.2% of QA jobs publish pay at all. For a full breakdown by role, read our QA engineer salary report.

Experience: how senior is a test manager job?

18 ads state a minimum number of years. The median is 8 years, and 8 of the 18 ask for 10 years or more. Netomi, Zayo Group and CACI ask for 12 years, while SOFTGAMES and PTC ask for 5.

Where are the remote test manager jobs?

29 of the 34 jobs are remote, which is close to the board-wide 90.9%. But remote rarely means anywhere. By location, the jobs break down like this:

  • Europe: 12. Three are open across the EU or Europe (Natuvion and two SOFTGAMES roles), three are in Switzerland (all for clients in Bern), two in Germany, and one each in Hungary, Portugal, Cyprus and the UK.
  • United States: 10, all remote. Concept Plus requires US citizenship.
  • India: 6, including Bosch in Bangalore and Experian in Hyderabad.
  • Canada and Australia: 2 each. The Australian government role at Softtest is for Australian citizens only.
  • Other: 2. Endava's Salesforce role is hybrid in Monterrey, Mexico, and BTSE's Principal QA Engineer / QA Manager requires fluent Mandarin.

Language can be the real filter. 6 of the 34 ads are written in German, and Natuvion and TAP ask for very good German. Our guide to QA jobs in Germany covers which roles work in English. For more remote options at every level, see remote QA jobs.

How to get a test manager job

  • Show the strategy, not only the plan. 21 of 31 ads talk about owning a test or quality strategy. Describe one you wrote and what changed because of it.
  • Keep your hands on automation. 21 ads mention it. Show a framework you introduced or a manual-to-automated shift you led.
  • Pick a lane. Enterprise programme work (SAP, Workday, Oracle) and product QA management need different CVs. Write one for the lane you want.
  • Look at who hires testers at scale. Our best software companies to work for page ranks employers by open QA roles each day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a test manager earn?

In the US, test manager and QA manager ads with a yearly range have a median midpoint of about $164,000, based on 6 ads. Across all QA job ads, lead and manager titles have a median of $136,000. In Europe, published ranges in our sample run from €64,400 to €101,200.

Are there remote test manager jobs?

Yes, 29 of the 34 software test manager jobs in our sample are remote. Almost all are limited to one country or region, such as the US, the EU or Switzerland. Across the whole board, 90.9% of QA jobs are remote.

What is the difference between a test manager and a QA manager?

A test manager usually runs testing for a project or programme, often an enterprise system like SAP or Workday. A QA manager usually leads a QA team inside a product company and stays closer to automation. Our sample has 16 Test Manager titles and 9 QA Manager titles.

Do test managers need ISTQB certification?

Not usually, but it helps in Europe. 7 of 31 test manager ads mention ISTQB, and two Swiss roles require it. US and product company ads rarely ask for it.

How many years of experience does a test manager need?

Most ads ask for about 8 years. That is the median of the 18 ads that state a minimum, and 8 of them ask for 10 years or more.

Where can I find test manager jobs?

QA Job Board has a dedicated page for test manager jobs, updated daily from company career pages and job sources. You can also browse QA lead jobs if you are moving up from a lead role.

Methodology

We took all open jobs in the Test Manager category of QA Job Board's public jobs feed on 22 November 2026, kept those posted since 24 August 2026, and removed non-software quality roles, reposts, an expired ad and a test listing (34 jobs left). We searched the titles and full descriptions for skills, and the salary median uses the midpoint of the six US ads with a yearly range; one hourly range and one "up to" figure were left out. Board-wide figures (1,142 jobs, category counts, remote share, salary disclosure and the salary medians) come from our daily insights file built from the same sources.
