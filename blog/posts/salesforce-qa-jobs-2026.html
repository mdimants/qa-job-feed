Salesforce QA is a small but steady niche. In the last 60 days, 37 QA job posts on QA Job Board had "Salesforce" in the title, from 32 different employers. Almost all of them are remote, close to half are in the United States, and the median advertised salary is about $110,000 a year (from 13 posts with a USD range). What sets these jobs apart is not the test framework. It is platform knowledge: Apex, Flows, Sales Cloud and Service Cloud, and often a Salesforce certification.

How many Salesforce QA jobs are there?

Out of 1,146 open QA jobs on the board today, Salesforce testing is a specialist slice. For the detailed numbers in this article we used our published job file: 37 posts with Salesforce in the job title were published between 24 September and 23 November 2026. A wider net catches more: 101 of the 1,855 posts published in the same 60 days mention Salesforce somewhere in the title or description (5.4%). Many of those are general QA roles at companies whose product runs on, or integrates with, Salesforce.

The titles vary a lot. You will see "Salesforce QA Analyst", "Salesforce Tester", "Salesforce Test Engineer", "SDET (Salesforce)", "Salesforce QA Automation Developer" and "Lead Salesforce Testing". If you only search for "Salesforce QA Engineer", you will miss most of them. The easiest way is to search all Salesforce QA jobs, which matches every variation.

Who is hiring Salesforce testers?

Two groups of employers stand out.

  • US government contractors. Steampunk, ICF, Cherokee Federal, FormativGroup, ASI Partners, TISTA and VivSoft all have Salesforce tester roles on public sector projects. Ten of the 37 posts (27.0%) mention public trust, a security clearance or the UK BPSS check. These jobs often require US citizenship or residency, so read the eligibility line first.
  • Consultancies and Salesforce product companies. Endava, Valtech, Inspired Testing, Sequoia Connect and Aquiva hire Salesforce testers for client projects. Companies such as Nesto, Alcumus, Corpay, JFrog and Blue Cross & Blue Shield of Rhode Island hire them to test their own Salesforce setup.

A few things are worth pointing out.

  • Integration and regression testing lead. Salesforce orgs connect to billing, ERP and support tools, and every Salesforce release (three a year) can break custom code. That is why integration (64.9%) and regression (62.2%) show up more often than any tool.
  • Apex and Flows matter more than you think. You do not need to write Apex like a developer, but 37.8% of posts mention it and 43.2% mention Flows. Being able to read a trigger or a Flow and work out what to test is a real advantage.
  • Selenium share is the same as the overall market. Selenium appears in 29.7% of Salesforce QA posts, almost exactly its 30.1% share across all QA jobs. Playwright (21.6%) and Postman (21.6%) are slightly below their market shares of 25.2% and 22.0%.
  • Salesforce-specific tools are a bonus. Copado (21.6%) and Provar (10.8%) appear in a minority of posts. If you have used either, put it near the top of your resume. You can search jobs that mention Copado to see who uses it.
  • AI is arriving. Four posts already mention Agentforce, Salesforce's AI agent product. It is too early to call it a trend, but testing AI features inside Salesforce is likely to grow.

Manual or automation?

Both. 16 posts (43.2%) mention manual testing and 14 (37.8%) mention UAT, which is often done together with business users. At the same time, titles like "Salesforce QA Automation Developer" and "SDET (Salesforce)" show there is a clear automation track. Many Salesforce QA jobs are close to QA analyst jobs: you write test cases from user stories, run regression before releases and sign off with the business. How much do Salesforce QA jobs pay?

Thirteen of the 37 posts give a salary range in US dollars. The median midpoint is $110,000 a year. The lowest minimum is $65,205 and the highest maximum is $155,000. Thirteen posts is a small sample, so treat this as a rough guide, not a benchmark.

For comparison, the median advertised salary across all QA jobs with a disclosed salary is $112,000 (212 posts), and the median for QA Analyst roles is $93,000 (20 posts). So Salesforce QA pays roughly like a general QA engineer and noticeably more than a typical QA analyst, even though much of the day-to-day work is similar. Platform knowledge is what closes that gap. Most of the posts with salaries are US government contractor roles, which tend to publish ranges, so pay outside the US is likely to be lower.

How to get a Salesforce QA job

  1. Learn the platform for free. Create a free Salesforce Developer Edition org and work through Trailhead modules on Sales Cloud, Service Cloud and Flows. Write test cases for a lead-to-opportunity process you build yourself.
  2. Get one certification. 40.5% of posts mention a Salesforce certification. The Salesforce Administrator certification is the usual starting point for testers.
  3. Learn to read Apex and SOQL. You should be able to query records with SOQL to check test data and understand what an Apex trigger does.
  4. Add one automation tool. Selenium or Playwright plus Postman for API tests covers most posts. Copado or Provar is a plus.
  5. Show release testing experience. Mention regression cycles, sandbox refreshes and UAT sign-off on your resume. These are the words recruiters look for.

When you are ready, browse remote QA jobs or set up a search for Salesforce QA jobs and check it weekly. New posts appear every few days.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Salesforce QA engineer earn?

The median advertised salary for Salesforce QA jobs on QA Job Board is about $110,000 a year. That figure comes from 13 posts with a USD range, most of them in the United States. The overall QA median is $112,000.

Is Salesforce QA a good career?

Yes, if you like business processes as much as testing. Salesforce QA pays close to general QA engineering, and 91.9% of the posts we found are remote. The niche is small, so combine it with general QA skills.

Do Salesforce testers need to know coding?

Some coding helps but is not always required. 37.8% of Salesforce QA posts mention Apex and 18.9% mention SOQL, usually for reading code and checking data rather than writing features. Automation roles also expect Selenium, Playwright or similar.

Which certification is best for a Salesforce tester?

Most testers start with the Salesforce Administrator certification. 40.5% of Salesforce QA posts mention a Salesforce certification. It shows you understand objects, permissions and Flows, which is what you test every day.

Are Salesforce QA jobs remote?

Almost all of them are. 34 of the 37 Salesforce QA posts we analysed are remote, compared with 90.7% across all QA jobs. Many US government roles are remote but require US residency.

Where can I find Salesforce QA jobs?

You can find them on QA Job Board by searching for Salesforce, which covers titles like Salesforce Tester, Salesforce QA Analyst and Salesforce SDET. For related roles, browse QA analyst jobs.
